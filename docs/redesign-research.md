# Portfolio research and application

Reviewed 3 October 2026.

## Sources

- [Nielsen Norman Group: 5 Steps to Creating a UX-Design Portfolio](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-design-portfolios/). This is design-portfolio research rather than a study of software-engineer hiring. Applicable principles: choose a small relevant selection, explain personal role and decisions, and make the story scannable. Application here: four case studies, personal ownership, reasoning and limitations, with software-specific evidence.
- [W3C WAI: Page Structure Tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/page-structure/). Application: semantic header/main/footer, meaningful heading hierarchy, labelled navigation and a keyboard skip link.
- [web.dev: Learn Responsive Design](https://web.dev/learn/design/). Application: fluid grids, content-driven breakpoints, narrow-screen stacking and actual responsive checks.
- [GitHub Docs: Pinning items to your profile](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/how-tos/profile-customization/pinning-items-to-your-profile). Application: editorial emphasis on a few inspectable repositories and merged PRs rather than a raw contribution count. No GitHub profile settings were changed.

The project selection, visual design and career timeline are editorial decisions based on Jhye's stated target. The sources do not guarantee hiring outcomes.
